# Model Specification v0.1

## Purpose

Explore how decentralized household and firm decisions generate aggregate labour,
price, output, consumption, and inequality dynamics.

## Time

One step represents one month.

## Sequence within each month

1. Policy transfer is set.
2. Period flows are reset.
3. Firms and unemployed households are matched.
4. Firms produce and pay wages.
5. Households receive wages and transfers.
6. Households buy from the lowest-price firm with inventory.
7. Firms update prices and wages and may exit.
8. Aggregate statistics are recorded.

## Known simplifications

- One homogeneous good
- No credit or money creation
- No firm entry
- No taxes
- No explicit expectations
- No input-output production network
- No spatial structure
- No central bank
- No AI/LLM decisions

Each additional mechanism should be added only when necessary for a specific research
question.
